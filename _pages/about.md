Hello, stranger! My name is Alejandro. I am a multi and interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of **natural language processing (NLP)**, **human-computer interaction**, **information visualization**, and **digital humanities**. I hold a **PhD in Computer Engineering (2020)** and a **Master's Degree in Intelligent Systems (2016)**, plus degrees in Computer Engineering and Software Engineering from the University of Salamanca.

In 2016, I joined the Visual Analytics and Information Visualization Group from the University of Salamanca in Spain. [VisUSAL](http://visusal.usal.es/) and later the [GRIAL](https://grial.usal.es/) Research Group in Interaction and e-Learning, where I completed my PhD thesis, _"Supporting Methodology Transfer in Visualization Research with Literature-Based Discovery and Visual Text Analytics"_.

After completion of my PhD, I have been awarded two competitive postdoctoral fellowships: a **Margarita Salas Postdoctoral Fellowship (2022-2023)**, and a **Juan de la Cierva Postdoctoral Contract (2024-2025)** in the [NLP & IR Research Group](https://nlp.uned.es/) at UNED. Since September 2025, I have been an **Ayudante Doctor (Assistant Professor)** in the department of Computer Languages and Systems at UNED.

I regularly serve as a reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, _Computational Linguistics_, IEEE VIS/TVCG and ACM CHI, among others, and have contributed to program and scientific committees in Digital Humanities and visualization (including [VIS4DH](https://vis4dh.dbvis.de/)).
