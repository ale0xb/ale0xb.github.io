Hello, stranger! My name is Alejandro. I am a multi and interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of **natural language processing (NLP)**, **human-computer interaction**, **information visualization**, and **digital humanities**. I hold a **PhD in Computer Engineering (2020)** and a **Master's Degree in Intelligent Systems (2016)**, plus degrees in Computer Engineering and Software Engineering from the University of Salamanca.

In 2016, I joined the Visual Analytics and Information Visualization Group [VisUSAL](http://visusal.usal.es/) and later the [GRIAL](https://grial.usal.es/) Research Group in Interaction and e-Learning, where I completed my PhD thesis, _"Supporting Methodology Transfer in Visualization Research with Literature-Based Discovery and Visual Text Analytics"_. I am also a member of the NLP & IR Research Group at UNED, contributing to language technologies, evaluation methodologies, and large language models.

I have been awarded two competitive postdoctoral fellowships: a **Margarita Salas Postdoctoral Fellowship (2022-2023)** at LINHD (UNED), and a **Juan de la Cierva Postdoctoral Contract (2024-2025)** focused on visual analytics for NLP explainability and fairness in the Department of Computer Languages and Systems at UNED. Since September 2025, I have been an **Ayudante Doctor (Assistant Professor)** in the same department.

My recent research covers robust statistical methodologies for NLP evaluation, annotator disagreement, interpretability of large language models, and uncertainty-aware analysis of textual and cultural data. I have also contributed to semantic modeling, text embedding analysis, linguistic variation, and computational approaches to culturally situated narratives.

In information visualization, I have designed and evaluated visual analytics systems for textual uncertainty, semantic exploration, historical lexicography, and corpus-based knowledge discovery, with work presented at IEEE VIS, VIS4DH, and Digital Humanities venues. These contributions led to my invitation to the 2023 Dagstuhl seminar _"Visualization and the Humanities: Towards a Shared Research Agenda"_.

My recent publications include works at CHI, ACL, EMNLP, and COLING, and journals such as _Knowledge-Based Systems_, _IEEE Access_, and _IEEE Computer Graphics & Applications_. As of early 2026, I have authored more than forty scientific publications and have 378 citations in Google Scholar, with collaborations across Europe, the USA, and Latin America.

In competitive research, I have participated in major funded initiatives, including **ODESIA (2022-2026)** under Spain's National AI Strategy, **CLS-Infra** (ERC, H2020-INFRAIA-2020-1, Grant Agreement 101004984), **PROVIDEDH** (CHIST-ERA, PCIN-2017-064), and **exploreAT!** (Austrian Academy of Sciences, 2016-2019). I also co-chaired the **ODESIA-Challenge 2024**, an international competition for evaluating large language models in Spanish.

I regularly serve as a reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, _Computational Linguistics_, IEEE VIS/TVCG and ACM CHI, among others, and have contributed to program and scientific committees in Digital Humanities and visualization (including [VIS4DH](https://vis4dh.dbvis.de/)).
